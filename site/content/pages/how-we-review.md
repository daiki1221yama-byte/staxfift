Our goal is simple: help you pick the right tool for **your** situation, not sell you the most expensive one.

## Our process

1. **Define who it's for.** Every review starts with the reader: a first-time blogger needs different hosting from a growing online store.
2. **Use the product ourselves.** Where possible we sign up, set it up and use it for real tasks. When we haven't tested something hands-on, we say so clearly.
3. **Check the fine print.** We read pricing pages, renewal rates, refund policies and privacy policies — the parts that often surprise people later.
4. **Compare against alternatives.** No product is reviewed in a vacuum. We compare it against direct competitors, including free options.
5. **Keep it updated.** We revisit our guides regularly and show the last-updated date on every article.

## What we look at

| Factor | What we ask |
| --- | --- |
| Value | Is the price fair — including renewals and add-ons? |
| Ease of use | Can a non-expert get set up without help? |
| Reliability | Does it work consistently? |
| Support | Can you reach a helpful human when something breaks? |
| Privacy & security | How is your data handled? |
