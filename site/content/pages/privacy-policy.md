This Privacy Policy explains how {{site.name}} ("we", "us") handles information when you visit {{site.url}} (the "Site").

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

**Usage data.** We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand which pages are read. This collects aggregated data such as page views, referring sites, country, browser and device type. It does not use cookies to track you across websites.

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type and the time of your request, for security and to keep the Site running.

## Affiliate links and third-party cookies

Some links on the Site are affiliate links (see our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). When you click one, you leave our Site and the partner or affiliate network may place cookies or use similar technologies on **their** site to record that you came from us. Those cookies are governed by the partner's own privacy policy, not this one. We do not receive your name, payment details or other personal information from partners — only aggregated reports about clicks and purchases.

## How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the Site, respond to messages, understand which content is useful, and protect the Site from abuse. **We do not sell your personal information.**

## Your choices and rights

- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email us at [{{site.email}}](mailto:{{site.email}}).

## Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Data retention

We keep emails for as long as needed to answer your question and for a reasonable period afterwards. Analytics data is retained by our analytics provider according to its own policies.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
