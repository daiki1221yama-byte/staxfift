{{site.name}} helps people choose online tools with confidence — web hosting, VPNs, password managers, business software and money apps.

Most "best of" lists online are written to sell, not to help. We do it differently:

- **We explain the category first.** Before recommending anything, we explain how the product type works and which features actually matter for you.
- **We show trade-offs.** Every tool has downsides. We name them.
- **We point to free options.** If a free tool does the job, we'll say so — even though we earn nothing from it.
- **We update our guides.** Prices and features change. Each article shows when it was last updated.
