{{site.name}} is an independent, reader-supported website. To keep our guides free, some of the links on this site are **affiliate links**. If you click one and then buy a product or sign up for a service, the company may pay us a commission.

## What this means for you

- **You never pay more.** The price is the same whether or not you use our link. Sometimes our partners offer readers a discount, and we'll tell you when that's the case.
- **Commissions don't decide our verdicts.** We recommend products because we think they're a good fit for a specific reader — not because they pay the most. We regularly mention free or cheaper alternatives, including ones that pay us nothing.
- **We label affiliate links.** Every article that may contain affiliate links shows a short disclosure near the top. Links that go through `/go/` on our site are affiliate links.

## Who we work with

We may participate in affiliate programs run by individual companies and by affiliate networks. Through these programs we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases or sign-ups.

If we take part in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, the following applies: *As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.*

## Sponsored content

We do not publish paid reviews. If we ever publish sponsored content, it will be clearly labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page.
