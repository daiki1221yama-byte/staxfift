This guide compares lemlist, a multichannel outreach platform known for personalization, with six popular alternatives: Instantly, Smartlead, Woodpecker, Reply.io, Apollo.io, and Mailshake. ## The real pain: outreach that has to work with a tiny team

If you're a founder, an SDR, or part of a lead-gen agency with one to twenty people, your outreach probably runs into the same walls:

- **Deliverability is fragile.** One badly warmed mailbox or a spike in bounces, and suddenly your carefully written sequence is going straight to spam.
- **Generic emails get ignored.** Buyers can spot a template from the subject line. Personalizing every message by hand doesn't scale.
- **Email alone isn't enough.** Many prospects respond faster on LinkedIn or to a quick call, but juggling separate tools for each channel creates chaos.
- **Finding leads takes forever.** Building lists from scratch, finding verified emails, and keeping data clean can eat more time than the outreach itself.
- **Replies get lost.** When responses land across several mailboxes and LinkedIn accounts, interested prospects wait too long for an answer.
- **Pricing is confusing.** Some tools charge per user, some per mailbox, some by contacts or credits. Comparing them fairly takes real effort.

A good cold outreach platform addresses as many of these as possible, so a small team can run campaigns that feel personal without burning their domains.

## How to choose the best cold email software: 5 criteria that last

These criteria don't depend on any single feature launch, so they stay useful even as products change.

### 1. Deliverability tools

Look for built-in email warm-up, sending limits, and tools to monitor inbox placement. If emails don't land in the inbox, nothing else matters. Check whether warm-up is included or sold as an add-on.

### 2. Multichannel sequences

Can one sequence combine email with LinkedIn steps, calls, or text messages? For many B2B buyers, a mix of channels gets more replies than email alone. Check which plan unlocks multichannel steps.

### 3. Personalization at scale

Custom fields, personalized images, AI-written lines, and A/B testing all help your messages feel less like templates. The goal is to sound like one person writing to another, even when you're sending hundreds of emails.

### 4. Lead data and list building

A built-in lead database and email finder can save hours of list building. Check how data is priced, since many tools use credits that run out.

### 5. Pricing model at your size

Decide whether per-user, per-mailbox, or per-contact pricing works best for you. A solo founder with ten mailboxes and an agency with five users and fifty mailboxes will get very different value from the same plan.

**The rule of thumb:** if your strategy depends on many mailboxes and pure email volume, start with Instantly. If your buyers respond better to a mix of personalized emails, LinkedIn touches, and calls, start with lemlist.

## How to get the most out of a 14-day free trial

Two weeks goes fast, especially since new mailboxes need time to warm up. Here's a simple plan.

1. **Days 1–2: Connect a mailbox and start warm-up.** Use a secondary domain rather than your main company domain. Turn on warm-up immediately so it has as much time as possible to build a sending reputation.
2. **Days 3–5: Build a small, targeted list.** Use the built-in database or your own data to create a list of a few dozen well-matched prospects. Quality beats quantity during a trial.
3. **Days 6–8: Write one multichannel sequence.** Combine two or three emails with a LinkedIn step or a call task. Personalize the first line of each email and test one variation of the subject line.
4. **Days 9–12: Send slowly and watch the inbox.** Keep daily volume low. Track replies, bounces, and how easy it is to handle responses from the unified inbox.
5. **Days 13–14: Calculate real cost.** Estimate users, mailboxes, LinkedIn accounts, and data credits for your full setup, then compare with the tools you use today.

By the end, you'll know whether the tool makes your outreach better or just faster.

## 5 mistakes teams make with cold outreach tools

**1. Sending from the main company domain.** If something goes wrong with deliverability, you don't want it to affect your everyday business email. Use separate sending domains.

**2. Skipping warm-up.** New mailboxes need time to build a reputation. Sending a full campaign on day one is one of the fastest ways to land in spam.

**3. Personalizing only the first name.** Real personalization references something specific to the prospect or their company. A first-name merge field isn't enough.

**4. Ignoring other channels.** Some prospects never answer email but accept LinkedIn connections or pick up the phone. A multichannel sequence gives you more chances to connect.

**5. Underestimating data costs.** Credits for lead data and enrichment can run out quickly. Estimate how many leads you'll need each month before choosing a plan.
